I am a Postdoctoral Research Associate at Purdue University, advised by Professor Elisa Bertino and Professor Ninghui Li. My research builds resilient, trustworthy AI architectures — certified robustness, robust transfer learning, security of multimodal foundation models, and adversarial robustness in distributed AI. I received my Ph.D. in Cyberspace Security from Xidian University in June 2025, advised by Professor Xiaofeng Chen. I am always open to discussion — feel free to reach out by email. More about me →
