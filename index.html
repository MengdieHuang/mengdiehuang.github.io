About me

Welcome to my homepage. I am currently a Postdoctoral Research Associate at Purdue University, working under the advisement of Professor Elisa Bertino and Professor Ninghui Li. I received my Ph.D. in Cyberspace Security from Xidian University in June 2025, advised by Professor Xiaofeng Chen, during which time I also conducted research as a Visiting Ph.D. student at Purdue University from 2022 to 2025.

My research primarily focuses on the security and robustness of machine learning models in critical domains, with the overarching goal of building resilient and trustworthy AI architectures. Specifically, I advance core research in certified robustness, robust transfer learning, the security of multimodal foundation models, and adversarial robustness in distributed AI systems. I have published extensively in premier security journals and conferences, including IEEE TDSC, ACM TOPS, ACM TIOT, AsiaCCS, and ESORICS, and I was the recipient of the Best Paper Award at TrustCom 2024. Furthermore, I actively serve the academic community as a reviewer for prestigious journals such as IEEE TDSC, TIFS, and TNNLS, and act as an Artifact Evaluation Committee member for top-tier security conferences including ACM CCS and NDSS.

If you are interested in my research or in need of any support, I am always open to discussion. You are welcome to reach out to me via email.
