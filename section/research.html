Research

My research builds robust AI as a foundation for trustworthy AI. I pursue it in two directions: the adversarial robustness of general-purpose AI models, and robust AI in security systems for network intrusion detection and IoT.

Representative Publications

TDSC 2026

TriXfer: Triple-Cross Transfer Attack on Vision Transformer–Adapted Downstream Classifiers

Adversarial vulnerabilities in pretrained Vision Transformers can propagate to the downstream classifiers built on them. TriXfer is the first untargeted triple-cross transfer attack: adversarial examples crafted on clean surrogate data transfer across model architectures, datasets, and label spaces at once, even when both the pretrained ViT and the downstream classifier are black boxes. Experiments across supervised, self-supervised, and vision-language backbones reveal persistent inherited vulnerabilities.

paper
TDSC 2025

CARD: Robustness-Preserving Transfer Learning for Network Intrusion Detection via Contrastive Adversarial Representation Distillation

Adversarially training robust network intrusion detectors demands abundant labeled data and large models, while standard fine-tuning and distillation transfer accuracy but not robustness. CARD transfers both robustness and performance from a robust source model to a target task that differs in data domain, model architecture, or both. With only 5% of the training data, it achieves 17.67% and 8.38% higher adversarial robust accuracy than adversarial fine-tuning and distillation.

paper
TOPS 2025

Dimensional Robustness Certification for Deep Neural Networks in Network Intrusion Detection Systems

Certified defenses give provable robustness guarantees, but existing ones target homogeneous image features and fit poorly to heterogeneous network traffic. MARS (Multi-order Adaptive Randomized Smoothing) certifies deep network intrusion detectors, enlarging the ℓ2 certified radius by 12.23% and improving detection under evasion attacks and natural corruptions. Its dimension-wise certified radii also reveal how robustness differs across traffic features. A shorter version received the Best Paper Award at TrustCom 2024.

paper
AsiaCCS 2023

Boost Off/On-Manifold Adversarial Robustness for Deep Learning with Latent Representation Mixup

Adversarial training yields attack-specific robustness that generalizes poorly to unseen attacks, and mixup has rarely been studied in latent space or against on-manifold attacks. LarepMixup interpolates disentangled latent representations in two modes, convex mixing and binary-mask mixing, to synthesize semantic samples, then trains with soft-label cross-entropy to refine the decision boundary. On CIFAR-10, SVHN, and ImageNet-Mixed10, it yields models robust to both off- and on-manifold adversarial attacks.

paper
JIT 2019

Sparse Selective Encryption for HEVC 4K Video Using Spatial Error Spread

Encrypting entire 4K video streams is too costly for delay- and power-constrained systems. Sparse Selective Encryption (SSE) encrypts only a few bits of the HEVC bitstream without coupling to the encoder, letting the error from encrypted units spread through the spatial correlation of prediction units. Two spatial influence models, based on angular intra prediction and motion vector prediction, sparsely select the key units, with adjustable encryption density and format compliance preserved.

paper
ICCT 2018

Selective Encryption of H.264/AVC based on Block Weight Model

The growth of 4K and 8K streaming calls for selective encryption with as low an encryption ratio as possible. For H.264/AVC, a block weight model exploits the reference structure of a video sequence to quantify how each block affects the motion compensation of others, so only the key bytes of the compressed bitstream are encrypted. The scheme resists both cryptographic and sketch attacks, and an adjustable parameter supports different security levels.
