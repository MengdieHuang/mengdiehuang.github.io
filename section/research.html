Research

My overarching goal is to build resilient, trustworthy AI architectures. I pursue it along four thrusts.

Research Areas

1

Certified Robustness and Vulnerability Assessment

Scalable certification for provable adversarial robustness guarantees, and realizable evasion attacks that expose vulnerabilities across network and image domains.

2

Robust Transfer Learning and Downstream Adaptation

Parameter-efficient training and representation distillation for robust downstream adaptation of foundation models against cross-domain threats.

3

Security of Multimodal Foundation Models

Hardening vision-language models against adversarial attacks and data extraction, with a focus on membership inference and robust VLM adaptation.

4

Adversarial Robustness in Distributed AI

Securing federated and decentralized learning against evasion attacks, and developing self-evolving defense mechanisms for distributed architectures.
